Word Quest – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Word Quest is a vocabulary quiz game for Android, made by Seyi Babs ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: there are no accounts, your progress stays on your phone, and the only outside service that receives data is Google AdMob, which shows the ads.

1. Information stored only on your phone

  • Game progress: your player name and avatar, levels, stars, points, coins, achievements, saved words and settings. These are kept in a database on your device. They are never sent to us.
  • Your age answer: the first time you open the app, it asks for your year of birth. The answer is kept on your phone and used only to decide which kind of ads, if any, you may see (see section 3). It is not sent to us or to Google.

We do not ask for your email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details, and we have no servers that collect your information.

2. Word packs

When your phone is online, the app may download new word packs from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), which hosts the files. As with any web request, GitHub receives your device's IP address to deliver the file. The app sends no personal information with this request. See GitHub's privacy statement.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Word Quest is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads appear only on menu screens and between levels, never while you are answering a question. Watching a video for extra coins is always optional.

  • Under 13: no ads are requested at all.
  • Ages 13–17: only non-personalised ads, with a family-friendly content rating, and ad requests are marked for users under the age of consent.
  • 18 and over: ads follow the choices you make in Google's consent message, where one is required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK). You can change these choices at any time under Settings → Ads & privacy → Privacy options.

When ads are shown, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud, and, where you allow it, to personalise them. Please see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings: Settings → Google → Ads (the wording may differ slightly between phones).

4. Children

Word Quest is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and players who say they are under 13 are not shown any ads. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Keeping or deleting your data

Because your progress is stored only on your phone, you are in control of it. To delete everything the app has stored, clear the app's storage (Settings → Apps → Word Quest → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the app. For information Google holds about ads, use the links in section 3.

6. Security

Word packs are downloaded over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and checked before they are used. Your game data never leaves your device.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app uses information, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
